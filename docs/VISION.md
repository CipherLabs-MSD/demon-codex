# Vision

## Labels used throughout this repository

- **CANON**: approved world/product facts supplied by the founder.
- **WORKING DECISION**: current implementation or process choice; revisable.
- **PROPOSAL**: future possibility, not an approved commitment.
- **UNKNOWN**: requires a decision; TODO is explicit unfinished work.
- **RESTRICTED**: material exists outside this repository; no secret payloads here.

CANON: Demon Codex is a dark-fantasy collectible universe with 666 canonical
demons. Its positioning is PLAY-TO-COLLECT. Normal gameplay requires no blockchain.
The product should be enjoyable on its own and avoid pay-to-win design.

PROPOSAL: an original mobile race/knockout game inspired by ancient
cross-and-circle games is the entry point. The broader IP may span lore, music,
cards, prints, apparel, statues, creators, social play and exceptional provenance.

WORKING DECISION: optimize clarity → modularity → testability → iteration → evidence.
Foundation v0.1 creates documentation and controls only; v0.01 names the future
first playable. These version labels describe different deliverables.

ACCEPTED: Unity 6 for M001 and the primary mobile client; see
[ADR 0002](adr/0002-unity-6-engine.md). iOS and Android are the primary platform direction.

UNKNOWN: audience, age positioning, final visual tone, device baseline,
launch territories and final rules. Do not invent answers to close documents.
