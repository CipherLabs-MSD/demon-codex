# Art bible

CANON: dark fantasy. UNKNOWN: final rendering style, tone/age limits, palettes,
silhouette grammar, faction motifs, camera, animation budget and mobile asset limits.

PROPOSAL: explore distinct silhouettes and readable board pieces before producing
large portrait sets. Briefs should record entity ID, purpose, silhouette, palette,
materials, symbols/sigil, accessibility considerations and approval state.
Track source, creator, rights/license, permitted uses and derivation for every asset.
Portrait, gameplay representation, animation, voice and musical motif are separate
media roles. Placeholder art must be labeled and must not become approved canon.
No generated art or character roster is required by this foundation.
