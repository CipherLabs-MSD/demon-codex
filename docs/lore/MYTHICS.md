# Mythics and anomalies

WORKING DECISION: Mythic is not an ordinary rarity. Hidden, Anomaly, Mythic, Unique
and Unnumbered are candidate special classifications independent of rarity and edition.

PROPOSAL: future MythicEncounterService supports probability, supply, knowledge,
temporal, achievement and discovery scarcity as separate policy dimensions.
First Witness, Original Witness, Early Witness and Witness are candidate historical
provenance states; exact definitions, exclusivity and transfer behavior are UNKNOWN.

Exceptional encounters need not be normal loot. Discovery history must be separate
from current ownership. Optional public certificates could establish bearer/history
for exceptionally scarce instances without making gameplay depend on a chain.
No encounter service, secret predicates or blockchain code is implemented.
