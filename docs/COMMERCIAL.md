# Commercial model

CANON: PLAY-TO-COLLECT; avoid designing around pay-to-win.
PROPOSAL: mobile distribution through Apple App Store and Google Play, web,
later Steam, physical commerce and licensing/IP.

Candidate revenue: skins, dice/board cosmetics, animation/VFX, season/event passes,
starter bundles, merchandise, licensing and optional exceptional provenance services.
All pricing, purchase entitlements and platform eligibility are UNKNOWN.

PROPOSAL: track gross revenue → platform/payment fees → tax → refunds → net
receipts → COGS → operating expenses → contribution margin. Finance must define
accounting categories, tax treatment and metric terminology before using this
conceptual waterfall for reporting; it is not accounting advice or a ledger design.

PROPOSAL: cards, prints, apparel, coins/medallions, figurines, statues and premium
artifacts may share an instance serial with digital collectibles. A future example
could pair canonical #006 / instance #0042 with physical artifact #0042.
QR, NFC, signed certificates or optional tokens may establish authenticity.
Redemption, custody, duplicate claims, returns and ownership transfer remain UNKNOWN.
No payments, fulfillment or minting are implemented. Legal/platform review must
precede commitments, supported by current authoritative requirements at that time.
